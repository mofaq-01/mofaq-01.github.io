## Research interests

- Arabic natural language processing and computational linguistics
- Large language models and Arabic language technologies
- Semantic and contextual analysis
- Arabic named-entity recognition, semantic role labeling, topic modeling, and argument mining
- Applied machine learning and artificial neural networks
- Data analytics and intelligent information retrieval
- Solar-energy forecasting and photovoltaic-system performance
- Reliable deployment of AI and research software on cloud-native platforms

## Research proposal

**Applying Artificial Neural Networks in Data Analytics to Improve the Prediction of Solar Generation and PV System Performance: The Role of Machine Learning in Reducing Solar Soft Costs**

This research direction explores how artificial-neural-network-based data analytics can predict solar generation and photovoltaic-system performance, including daily estimates, while helping reduce solar soft costs such as design fees, insurance, and taxes through earlier and more reliable assessment.

## Arabic AI and language technology

I am interested in developing and evaluating language technologies for Arabic text understanding using tools such as Farasa, CAMeL Tools, AraBERT, CAMeLBERT, Arabic OCR, and Python-based machine-learning workflows.
