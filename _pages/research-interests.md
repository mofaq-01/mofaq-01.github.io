## Research areas

Computational linguistics; Arabic natural language processing; semantic modelling; contextual analysis; information extraction; text classification; and argument analysis.

## Current research proposal

**Applying Natural Language Processing to the Semantic and Contextual Analysis of Classical Arabic Texts.**

## Research objective

To develop and evaluate an NLP based approach for analysing classical Arabic texts, with a focus on identifying semantic relationships, contextual meanings, rationales, and argumentative structures across differing positions and interpretations.
