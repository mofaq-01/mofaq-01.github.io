**Re my name** : Family and friends call me *Mowafi* (Mo-WAH-fee). My given name is **Mofaq**, and in English the Q is better left silent. **Al Faiz** is my grandfather’s name. Feel free to use either :-)

I am a Senior Software Engineer at Red Hat with more than seven years of experience in applied artificial intelligence, natural language processing, data engineering, and cloud-native computing. I have designed, evaluated, and deployed solutions across the banking, telecommunications, and healthcare industries, with professional experience at Red Hat and IBM.

I work with machine learning, natural language processing, Python, data analytics, IBM Watson, TensorFlow, GenAI, software containerization, automation, and cloud-native systems. I have delivered hands-on technical workshops and chaired sessions at IEEE, university, and industry events.

My interests include Arabic natural language processing, computational linguistics, machine learning, large language models, semantic analysis, contextual analysis, and information retrieval. My current research agenda focuses on developing and evaluating language technologies for Arabic text understanding.

I hold an **MSc in Computer Science** from the Catholic University of America in Washington, D.C., United States, School of Engineering and Computer Science, and a **BSc in Computer Science** from Taif University, School of Engineering and Computer Science. I also have industry experience as an R&D software engineer at IBM and Red Hat.
